Frequently Asked Questions

What motorcycles does Transport Bridle work with?

The site shows examples across sport, touring/cruiser and dirt/adventure-style motorcycles. Final fitment language should match your tested applications.

What if my handlebars have more than 3 inches of rise?

Use the alternate high-rise setup shown on the How It Works page and in Instruction Box #2.

How do I measure handlebar rise?

Measure vertically from the handlebar mount to the handlebar grip.

How do I connect my tie-down?

For the standard setup, the supplied instructions show the tie-down hook passing through the small end loop before returning to the anchor point.

Where can I see installation examples?

Visit How It Works and Videos for real motorcycle examples.
