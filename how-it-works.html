How Transport Bridle Works

A simple visual guide using real motorcycles.

Standard Setup

For sport, touring, adventure and dirt-bike style applications with 3 inches of rise or less.

Position the Bridle

Place the handlebar loops at the appropriate grip/handlebar locations.

Connect the Tie-Down

Pass the tie-down hook through the small end loop, then connect to the anchor point.

Secure the Bike

Apply tension evenly and confirm the motorcycle is centered and stable.

Important: For bikes with more than 3 inches of rise, mini apes or ape hangers, use the high-rise setup below and follow the detailed instructions supplied with the product.
